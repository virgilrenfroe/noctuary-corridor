Light Lab — a short module for intro physics, media-arts, and instructional design. Mullions occlude the beam into shafts. The lens bends what gets through into the pool. Dust in the beam is scatter. Desktop draws the shafts with an occlusion-map radial blur on one WebGL context. Narrow screens, a coarse pointer, or ?safe=1 skip EffectComposer and use additive shaft quads.
